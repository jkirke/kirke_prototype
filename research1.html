«Stakeholder Mapping»

This Stakeholder Mapping centers an individual senior staff software engineer and visualizes team and company wide relationships.

The first ring (pink) depicts the most immediate actors and team connections. These developers and managers continuously interact with the software engineer.

The second ring (blue) depicts actors related to, but not continuously interacting with the software engineer. These include other managers and teams which may or may not be involved in the development of a single product.

The third ring (green) depicts actors that do not interact with the software engineer, but have influence on the product cycle. These include both people (eg. customers) and concepts (eg. policies).

Additionally, the pink dashed lines depict task-based connections as opposed to collaborative or team-based connections.
