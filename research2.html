«Ecosystem Mapping»

This Ecosystem Mapping examines the various roles across the development and release of a single product. This visualization represents a generalized product cycle, as opposed to a specific product case study.

The Ecosystem Mapping differs from the Journey Mapping in terms of scope and focus.

The product cycle consists of the following stages: planning, requirement analysis, design, development, testing, deployment, maintenance, and retirement.

The first ring (pink) depicts the most immediate actors and team connections. These relationships span the entire product cycle.

The second ring (blue) depicts actors related to, but not continuously interacting with the product.

The third ring (green) depicts actors that do not interact directly with the product, but have influence on development. These include people (eg. customers) and concepts (eg. policies). Tools (eg. Jira) and artifacts (eg. support tickets) are highlighted in yellow.
