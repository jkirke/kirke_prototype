Agentic AI Intervention: Management and Automation

This map depicts a three-pronged path, highlighting the potential for agentic AI tools in the senior software engineer’s workflow. The top path, (green), represents the pre-AI adoption workflow. The middle path, (blue), represents the current workflow. Finally, the bottom path, (pink), represents a future workflow.

The targeted intervention points allow the engineer to focus on unique, product or situation dependent testing and security tasks. The agents must be easy for the engineer to understand, create, and deploy for various tasks. Integration with message systems allows the agents to work in the background and “ping” the engineer at task problems or at task completion.

Pre-AI adoption, the workflow took approximately 6 hours and 30 minutes. With current AI adoption, the workflow takes 40 minutes. With an agentic intervention, this workflow can be completed in the background with a final manual review.

This map serves as one particular example where agents may benefit the engineer’s workflow.
